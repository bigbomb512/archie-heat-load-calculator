# Stage 6 — Gated Dynamic Envelope Methods

Stage 6 adds deployable envelope methods without silently changing legacy
reports. Every advanced method is inactive until its project-local method gate
is approved and every input is explicit, cited, and current.

## Supported gated methods

- fixed adjacent-temperature partitions;
- fixed ground-contact floors;
- first-order hourly RC thermal mass;
- two-node same-hour room-to-room dynamic partition coupling;
- cited 24-hour surface-plane irradiance.

Dynamic methods reset their state at the beginning of each design-day scenario.
The coupling solver uses one owning partition record and reports equal-and-
opposite room transfers. Reciprocal duplicate surfaces, unresolved ownership,
missing initial state, invalid resistance/capacitance, missing citations, and
non-convergent iterations fail closed.

Radiation is accepted only from a cited, location/timezone/date-specific,
24-hour surface irradiance source with reviewed orientation. It is kept
separate from the existing manual solar basis and is never generated by a live
weather lookup, a render, or visual inference.

## Audit and freshness

Snapshots and reports retain method-gate versions, selected surface/coupling
IDs, initial-state data, convergence settings, radiation fingerprints, and
blocked-method reasons. Changing any of those dependencies makes the current
snapshot/report stale; historical artifacts remain unchanged.

## Benchmark boundary

The parity harness can compare a versioned benchmark case and report why a
comparison is blocked or incomplete. It does not claim CAMEL+ equivalence or
produce `validated`. An authorised benchmark release, independent input
reconciliation, and a later product decision are still required before that
status could exist.

## Deferred

Annual 8,760-hour weather, scenario-to-scenario state carry-over, generated
solar position, full direct/diffuse radiation modelling, soil dynamics, and
annual energy reporting remain outside this release.
