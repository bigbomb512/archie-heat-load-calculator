# Certificate wall, roof and north reading (23 November 2026)

`ai/certificate_constructions.py` reads the original PDF text layer on drawing pages whose inventory title says BASIX or NatHERS. It accepts only adjacent, labelled construction and insulation entries. Every accepted value retains the drawing page and a short excerpt. A bare `NORTH` label, compass direction in a window schedule, `Sarking`, or an unlabelled R-value supplies no numeric bearing or insulation value. Image-only certificate details remain outside this deterministic reader; the existing P8 image task still supplies window U/SHGC.

The provisional opaque U-value is `1 / total R`, using a one-dimensional series in the style of AIRAH Technical Handbook 2007 pp. 150–173. For a brick veneer wall, the assumed layers are indoor vertical film R0.12, 10 mm plasterboard (`k=0.17 W/mK`), the **printed** bulk insulation R, a 100 mm vertical cavity R0.16, 110 mm brick (`k=0.758`), and outdoor summer film R0.04. For a tiled roof over an insulated ceiling, they are indoor downward film R0.16, 10 mm plasterboard, printed ceiling insulation R, ventilated pitched roof space R0.46, 19 mm clay tile (`k=0.84`), and outdoor film R0.04. A separately printed roof insulation R is added. The source for these layer values is `config/stored_heat.json` (AIRAH 2007 page citations). The [ABCB housing energy efficiency handbook](https://www.abcb.gov.au/sites/default/files/resources/2024/Housing%20energy%20efficiency%20handbook.pdf) says wall total R is calculated in accordance with AS/NZS 4859.2, including framing bridges. This provisional series estimate does **not** resolve framing bridges, compression, penetrations, or installation quality and is not an AS/NZS 4859.2 compliance value. A construction reviewer should replace it with a confirmed total-R/U where those effects matter.

The certificate construction is inserted after the pack proposal but leaves a typed operator construction answer in place. Its description and page excerpt accompany the U-value into the calculation and stored-heat construction selection. A numeric certificate `Plan-up azimuth` may replace an AI estimate in the in-memory plan geometry used for calculation; a reviewer declaration takes precedence. Certificate values count as answered in assumption ranges.

Saved Turramurra jobs with BASIX page 2 have `Brick Veneer`, `Insulation External Walls R2.0`, `Ceilings with cavity to roof R4.0`, and roof material `Tiles`. The same page says roof insulation `Sarking` without an R number and has a `NORTH` label without an azimuth. The Todman Avenue saved jobs' page 3 is a BASIX-titled architectural title page without these constructions. No saved job supplies a numeric certificate north bearing through this reader.
